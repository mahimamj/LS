Our Journey & Commitment

Lubricant Solutions has been a pillar of the industrial community in Ludhiana and across Punjab for over six decades. Founded on the values of absolute integrity and reliable service, we have transitioned through three generations of family leadership, expanding our capabilities to serve over 500 industrial plants today.

As authorized partners for Bharat Petroleum Corporation Ltd. (MAK Lubricants), Gandhar Oil Refinery India Ltd. (Divyol Lubricants), and Generation Four Engitech Ltd. (Hi-Tech Lubricants), we offer a comprehensive range of industrial lubricants, gear oils, hydraulic fluids, metalworking coolants, and specialty greases designed for high-stress manufacturing environments.

1963

Our foundation in Ludhiana's industrial core, building the roots of industrial lubricant supply chain trust.

2019

Established as Lubricant Solutions, partnering as authorized distributors for BPCL (MAK Lubricants).

Present

Supporting over 500 manufacturing, steel, and hosiery plants with technical-first fluid management and reliable supplies.
