Sample Testing

Use lubricant analysis to make maintenance decisions based on evidence instead of guesswork.

How it works

Oil and fluid samples are reviewed to detect contamination, wear indicators, oxidation, viscosity shifts, and other warning signs that affect reliability.

Benefits

  • Early warning of lubrication and machine health issues
  • More informed drain interval and maintenance planning
  • Reduced cost from avoidable failures and fluid misuse

Why it matters

Lubricants carry valuable information about both fluid condition and equipment behavior. Sample testing helps convert that data into practical action.
