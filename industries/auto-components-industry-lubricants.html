Can coolant management improve tool life?

Yes. Proper concentration, contamination control, and fluid selection can improve cooling, lubricity, and machining consistency.

What hydraulic oil suits production equipment?

The correct fluid depends on pressure, temperature, pump design, and cleanliness targets. Anti-wear, high VI oils are commonly evaluated.
