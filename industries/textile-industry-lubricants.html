Textile industry lubricants for continuous-duty performance

Textile operations rely on smooth-running gear drives, compressors, hydraulic systems, and utility assets. Lubrication quality directly affects uptime, fabric quality, and maintenance planning.

Industry challenges

  • Continuous-duty gearboxes operating under heat and long shift cycles
  • Dust, lint, and contamination affecting lubricant cleanliness
  • Compressor and hydraulic reliability issues disrupting production

Recommended lubrication solutions

We typically support textile plants with gear oils, hydraulic oils, compressor oils, and bearing greases chosen around duty, temperature, and contamination risk.

Services offered

Condition monitoring, sample testing, filtration advice, and technical troubleshooting help textile facilities improve lubricant life and cut avoidable failures.

Which lubricant is best for loom gearboxes?

The right answer depends on load, temperature, speed, and OEM requirements. EP or synthetic gear oils are often considered for high-duty service.

Can lubricant selection reduce gearbox temperature?

Yes. Correct viscosity and base oil choice can reduce friction losses and improve thermal stability when matched properly to the application.

Do you help with troubleshooting recurring wear?

Yes. We support application review, product recommendation, and service inputs to address recurring wear and lubrication mistakes.
