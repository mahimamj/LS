Industry Solutions

Plastic and injection molding lubricants for stable machine cycles

Injection molding and plastic processing plants need hydraulic stability, compressor reliability, and bearing protection to maintain repeatable production and part quality.

Industry challenges

  • Hydraulic heat buildup and unstable response in molding machines
  • Compressed air reliability for plant support systems
  • Bearing performance in heat-affected or high-speed support equipment

Recommended lubrication solutions

We commonly support hydraulic oils, compressor oils, and greases matched to molding cycles, ambient conditions, and asset criticality.

Services offered

Technical support, sample testing, and filtration guidance help improve machine stability and reduce heat- or contamination-related lubrication issues.

Can the wrong hydraulic oil affect molding consistency?

Yes. Poor viscosity fit, oxidation instability, or air entrainment can affect system response and heat behavior.

What grease works for ancillary bearings?

The right grease depends on speed, load, and temperature. We help match grease type to the actual bearing condition.

Do you help with oil cleanliness improvement?

Yes. Filtration practices and fluid condition reviews are often part of improving hydraulic system reliability.
