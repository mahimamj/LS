Industry Solutions

Steel and metal industry lubricants for severe-duty environments

Steel and metal operations demand lubricants that can withstand heat, shock loads, water ingress, and contamination without compromising equipment reliability.

Industry challenges

  • High temperature and thermal stress near furnaces and rolling operations
  • Shock loading in gear systems and exposed process equipment
  • Contamination from scale, dust, and moisture

Recommended lubrication solutions

Common solution areas include synthetic or EP gear oils, high-performance hydraulic oils, severe-duty greases, and specialty lubricants for extreme conditions.

Services offered

We support sample testing, filtration services, condition monitoring, and technical selection guidance to reduce wear, overheating, and premature lubricant degradation.

Do steel plants need synthetic gear oil?

Many severe-duty applications benefit from synthetic gear oils, but the right choice depends on load, temperature, and maintenance goals.

How can contamination be reduced?

Better sealing, fluid handling practices, and filtration support often make a major difference in lubricant health and component life.

Can you help with hydraulic reliability issues?

Yes. We help review fluid fit, cleanliness, oxidation stability, and service practices for steel plant hydraulics.
