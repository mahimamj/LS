Synthetic vs mineral oil - complete guide

Both synthetic and mineral oils have a place in industrial lubrication. The right choice depends on operating conditions, reliability targets, and maintenance strategy.

Where synthetic oils stand out

Synthetic lubricants often offer stronger oxidation resistance, better thermal stability, cleaner operation, and longer service life in demanding environments.

Where mineral oils still fit well

Mineral oils can be a practical choice for moderate-duty applications where operating conditions are stable and drain interval expectations are more conservative.

How to decide

Consider temperature, load, cleanliness, operating hours, cost of downtime, and whether extended performance can offset higher upfront price.
