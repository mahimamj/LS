Specialty Lubricants for difficult industrial operating conditions

When standard lubricants are not enough, we help identify specialty formulations for heat, contamination, food-safe requirements, or challenging duty cycles.

Description

Specialty lubricants are chosen where equipment needs more than routine protection, including high-temperature bearings, shock-loaded gears, and application-sensitive systems.

Applications

  • Severe-duty gear and bearing locations
  • High-temperature process lines
  • Special operating environments needing tailored chemistry

Benefits

  • Supports reliability in hard-to-lubricate applications
  • Can improve drain life and reduce maintenance frequency
  • Helps solve persistent lubrication-related failure modes

Available brands

MAK (BPCL), Divyol (Gandhar), Castrol, HPCL, Shell, Mobil, Servo, Balmerol, Valvoline, and other specialty solutions selected case by case.
