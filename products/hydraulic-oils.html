Hydraulic Oils for smooth, clean, and reliable power transmission

Anti-wear, high VI, and application-focused hydraulic fluids for production equipment, presses, molding systems, and industrial power packs.

Description

Hydraulic oil performance depends on viscosity fit, cleanliness, oxidation resistance, and foam control. We help plants choose the right fluid for stable response and longer component life.

Applications

  • Power packs and hydraulic presses
  • Injection molding machines
  • Process and utility hydraulics

Benefits

  • Improved pump and valve protection
  • Lower risk of heat buildup and erratic response
  • Better cleanliness and oxidation stability

Available brands

MAK (BPCL), Divyol (Gandhar), Castrol, HPCL, Shell, Mobil, Servo, Balmerol, Valvoline, and compatible premium options aligned to system conditions.
