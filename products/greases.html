Greases for bearings, motors, and loaded industrial points

Grease selection affects bearing temperature, sealing performance, relubrication intervals, and equipment life. We help plants get that choice right.

Description

From lithium complex and EP grease to specialty high-temperature options, we recommend greases based on speed, load, environment, and relubrication needs.

Applications

  • Electric motor bearings
  • Fans, blowers, conveyors, and couplings
  • Process equipment in dusty or high-load conditions

Benefits

  • Stable lubrication film and wear control
  • Improved resistance to water, heat, and mechanical working
  • Lower risk of over-greasing and misapplication issues

Available brands

MAK (BPCL), Divyol (Gandhar), Castrol, HPCL, Shell, Mobil, Servo, Balmerol, Valvoline, and other fitting grease technologies for plant conditions.
